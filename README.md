Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-steady-ibis-48-6610-the-worst-line-an-ai-could-say-2ae5e90f): the worst pickup line an AI could say, in 140 characters or fewer.

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The line

> I analyzed 4.2 million pickup lines and picked this one. You have 10 seconds to say yes before I time out and try someone else.

## Run
